### Rationale, one sentence each

1. **Persona** - Two women's identities blur until the film itself seems to tear and burn; the most formally radical film he made and the highest-placed Bergman in the 2022 Sight and Sound poll (18th).
2. **The Seventh Seal** - A knight plays chess with Death across a plague-ridden Sweden; its images became shorthand for art-house cinema, and it leads this list on IMDb (8.1 from 219,144 votes) and RT review count (71).
3. **Wild Strawberries** - An old professor's road trip through memory and dream, carried by Victor Sjostrom; the humane counterweight to the austerity of the early canon.
4. **Fanny and Alexander** - A late summation: a child's view of a wealthy theatrical family and a cruel stepfather, with 100% on RT and one of his three Foreign Language Oscar winners.
5. **Cries and Whispers** - Three sisters and a maid in a house drenched in red, with Sven Nykvist's colour photography the film's argument; its Oscar was for cinematography.
6. **Through a Glass Darkly** - A four-person chamber drama on a Baltic island that opened the faith trilogy and won the Foreign Language Oscar; 100% on RT from 27 reviews.
7. **The Virgin Spring** - A medieval ballad of crime and revenge told with stark austerity; another of his three Foreign Language Oscar winners and 8.0 on IMDb.
8. **Smiles of a Summer Night** - The comedy of manners that first brought him international attention and later became the basis of the musical A Little Night Music; shows the range of the filmography.
9. **Scenes from a Marriage** - The disintegration of a marriage in close two-handed scenes, with an IMDb rating of 8.3; ranked low because of its television origin and the thin sample for this cut (10,783 votes, 23 RT reviews).
10. **Autumn Sonata** - Ingrid Bergman and Liv Ullmann in a long mother-daughter reckoning, the only film to unite Ingrid Bergman with Ingmar Bergman; the closest call over Winter Light, Shame and Hour of the Wolf.

### Credit, format and version flags

- **Fanny and Alexander (1982)**: Sole director per TMDB crew (Director, Writer). TMDB 5961 is the 188-minute theatrical version; the five-hour television version is a separate TMDB entry (TV 33153) and was not used.
- **Scenes from a Marriage (1974)**: Sole director per TMDB crew (Director, Writer). Theatrical (169 minute) cut of a six-part television series (TMDB TV 65170, 1973; IMDb tt0070644, 8.5 from 17,954 votes). The film version is ranked here; TMDB, IMDb and RT all list it as a separate film entry. Flagged as a television-origin work.

**Near misses** (considered, not included):

- **Winter Light** (1963), TMDB 29455, IMDb tt0057358: Completes the faith trilogy with Through a Glass Darkly; IMDb 8.0 but RT 80% from 25 reviews, below Autumn Sonata on the critic line.
- **Shame** (1968), TMDB 26372, IMDb tt0063611: Strong war drama, but RT 68% from 25 reviews.
- **Hour of the Wolf** (1968), TMDB 18333, IMDb tt0063759: Distinct horror turn; overlaps Persona's psychological ground.
- **Summer with Monika** (1953), TMDB 47735, IMDb tt0046345: Influential on the French New Wave but not stronger than the ten; RT page not read.
- **Sawdust and Tinsel** (1953), TMDB 47721, IMDb tt0045848: Important early work; only 11 RT reviews.
- **The Silence** (1963), TMDB 11506, IMDb tt0057611: Completes the faith trilogy; not selected over the ten.

## Ranking method

The method is the one set out in [`ESSENTIAL_10_EXAMPLE.md`](./ESSENTIAL_10_EXAMPLE.md#ranking-method) and was applied unchanged: eligibility from TMDB crew credits, an evidence panel of IMDb rating and votes, RT Tomatometer and review count and a note on directing, tiers by directing achievement, order within a tier by written editorial judgement, and the top three flagged as signature films. IMDb and RT are never averaged or combined and are used only as ordinal evidence within this director's own filmography. Suggested sample floors are 10,000 IMDb votes and 20 RT reviews; below those a score is shown but carries little weight. Two additions follow the owner's decisions: documentaries and co-directed films are allowed where essential and flagged, and the list is shorter than ten where ten would be padding.

### Where the evidence and the order disagree (all deliberate)

- **Persona is #1 although The Seventh Seal has the higher IMDb rating and more votes** (8.1 from 219,144 against 8.0 from 142,942) and the higher Tomatometer (93% against 91%). Reason: Persona is the film that most changed what cinema could do with form and it is 18th in the 2022 Sight and Sound poll against 136th for The Seventh Seal. If the owner prefers the most recognisable Bergman, swap #1 and #2; the gap is small.
- **Scenes from a Marriage has the second-highest IMDb rating in the ten (8.3) but sits #9.** Reason: it is a trimmed television drama with a thin sample, so it carries less weight than the films made for the cinema.
- **Autumn Sonata (#10) has the weakest RT of the ten (84% from 32 reviews)** but 8.1 on IMDb from 44,012 votes; kept over Winter Light (80% from 25 reviews) because its performances are the stronger case and the evidence on both lines is marginally better.

**Known uncertainties**

- Scenes from a Marriage exists as a TV series and a theatrical cut; the film entries (TMDB 133919, IMDb tt6725014, RT /m/scenes_from_a_marriage, 2h 48m) were used throughout. The IMDb series entry (tt0070644) has a higher vote count and rating (8.5 from 17,954); do not mix the two.
- Fanny and Alexander has a theatrical cut (used) and a five-hour TV cut (TMDB TV 33153); the RT page shows a 3h 8m runtime, matching the theatrical cut.
- RT pages for The Virgin Spring (shown as 1959) and others show US release years; the ledger uses the original release year.
- RT pages for Summer with Monika, The Silence and The Magician were not read because those films are not in the ten; they were not needed for the decision.
- Runtime gaps between TMDB and IMDb: Autumn Sonata 93 vs 99 minutes, Cries and Whispers 95 vs 92; both are the same film (single entry each).
- Review counts and vote counts move daily; these are snapshots.
- TMDB film pages carry no IMDb link, so IMDb ids were matched by title, year and runtime; confirm via the TMDB API `external_ids` field when implementing.
- Alternate titles in the ledger come from IMDb primary and original titles and the TMDB display titles, not from TMDB's alternative-titles tab.
