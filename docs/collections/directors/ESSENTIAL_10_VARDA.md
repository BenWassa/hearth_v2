### Rationale, one sentence each

1. **Cléo from 5 to 7** - A singer awaits a medical result across ninety minutes of Paris afternoon, in near real time; the highest-placed Varda in the 2022 Sight and Sound poll (14th) and her most-watched film on IMDb (32,645 votes).
2. **Vagabond** - A young drifter's last weeks reconstructed from the testimony of people who met her; Golden Lion at Venice 1985 and 100% from 26 RT reviews.
3. **The Gleaners and I** - A documentary essay on foragers and waste that made her own filming a subject; =67 in the 2022 Sight and Sound poll and 95% from 44 RT reviews.
4. **Faces Places** - A road film in which Varda and JR paste portraits on village walls; Oscar-nominated for Best Documentary Feature and the strongest critic result of the list (99% from 143 reviews).
5. **Le Bonheur** - A sunlit story of a happy marriage that turns disturbing by form alone; =152 in the 2022 Sight and Sound poll and the best-known of her 1960s fiction after Cléo.
6. **The Beaches of Agnès** - A self-portrait told through mirrors, beaches and staged memories; the highest IMDb rating in the nine (8.0, from 5,463 votes), with 97% from 71 RT reviews.
7. **La Pointe Courte** - Her debut, a married couple's quarrel set against a fishing village, edited by Alain Resnais and widely seen as an advance sign of the French New Wave.
8. **Daguerréotypes** - A portrait of the shopkeepers on her own street, Rue Daguerre; the template for the observational documentary mode she returned to for forty years. Evidence is thin (2,362 IMDb votes, 11 RT reviews).
9. **One Sings, the Other Doesn't** - Two women's friendship over some fifteen years, linked to the women's movement and abortion rights; the clearest statement of her political fiction, though only 65% on RT.

## Ranking method

The method is the one set out in [`ESSENTIAL_10_EXAMPLE.md`](./ESSENTIAL_10_EXAMPLE.md#ranking-method) and was applied unchanged: eligibility from TMDB crew credits, an evidence panel of IMDb rating and votes, RT Tomatometer and review count and a note on directing, tiers by directing achievement, order within a tier by written editorial judgement, and the top three flagged as signature films. IMDb and RT are never averaged or combined and are used only as ordinal evidence within this director's own filmography. Suggested sample floors are 10,000 IMDb votes and 20 RT reviews; below those a score is shown but carries little weight. Two additions follow the owner's decisions: documentaries and co-directed films are allowed where essential and flagged, and the list is shorter than ten where ten would be padding.

### Where the evidence and the order disagree (all deliberate)

- **The list has nine films, not ten.** After the first nine, the next candidates (Varda by Agnès, Jacquot de Nantes, Kung-Fu Master!, Documenteur) either cannot be tied cleanly to IMDb and RT or have fewer than 3,000 votes and under 10 reviews; adding one would be padding. Varda by Agnès is the first reserve if the owner wants a tenth.
- **Four of the nine are documentaries and one is co-directed (Faces Places with JR).** The owner approved including both where essential and flagging them. They are flagged in the ledger as `format` and `directorCreditNote`.
- **Faces Places (RT 99% from 143 reviews, the best of the list) is #4, below The Gleaners and I (95%, 44 reviews) in the signature three.** Reason: The Gleaners and I is =67 in the 2022 Sight and Sound poll, defines her documentary voice, and is her solo work; Faces Places is a joint credit. If the owner prefers evidence over this judgement, swap #3 and #4.
- **Vagabond (7.6 on IMDb) and Le Bonheur (7.6) trail Cléo (7.8)**, and the strongest IMDb figure (8.0) belongs to The Beaches of Agnès from only 5,463 votes. The order follows direction and canon, not these scores.

**Known uncertainties**

- Four of the nine are documentaries and one is co-directed; this matches the owner's decision to include them where essential.
- La Pointe Courte is dated 1955 (festival and IMDb) here, 1956 on TMDB and RT. Daguerréotypes is dated 1975 here; TMDB gives 1978, RT 1976.
- La Pointe Courte has no Tomatometer on RT (4 reviews): null with reason, not zero.
- Varda by Agnès (2019) could not be cleanly tied to an IMDb feature entry (IMDb lists it as a miniseries, tt9530198, 7.8 from 2,872 votes, plus a separate tt43684922 'Causerie' entry), so it is a reserve rather than a ranked film.
- The IMDb dataset marks The Gleaners and I as the 2000 film; the two-years-later follow-up (TMDB 84026) is a separate short film.
- Review counts and vote counts move daily; these are snapshots.
- TMDB film pages carry no IMDb link, so IMDb ids were matched by title, year and runtime; confirm via the TMDB API `external_ids` field when implementing.
- Alternate titles in the ledger come from IMDb primary and original titles and the TMDB display titles, not from TMDB's alternative-titles tab.
