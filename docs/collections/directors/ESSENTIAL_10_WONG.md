### Rationale, one sentence each

1. **In the Mood for Love** - Two neighbours in 1962 Hong Kong, their restraint conveyed through repeated passages, slow motion and a recurring musical theme; fifth in the 2022 Sight and Sound poll and first on both IMDb (8.0 from 192,928 votes) and RT review count (191).
2. **Chungking Express** - Two interlocking stories of lovesick Hong Kong policemen shot in handheld bursts and step-printed colour, made quickly between larger projects; second on IMDb (7.9 from 115,106 votes) and the film that made his style widely imitated.
3. **Days of Being Wild** - The first full statement of his themes and visual manner (1960s Hong Kong, missed connections, cinematography by Christopher Doyle) that In the Mood for Love later continues; 91% on RT from 32 reviews.
4. **Happy Together** - A volatile relationship between two Hong Kong men in Buenos Aires, filmed in saturated colour and black and white; Best Director at Cannes 1997.
5. **2046** - A sequel in spirit to In the Mood for Love, folding a science-fiction story into the memory of a writer; 7.4 on IMDb from 65,225 votes and 87% from 119 RT reviews.
6. **Fallen Angels** - Originally conceived as a third story for Chungking Express, then made as a separate film, shot with ultra-wide lenses and neon, at 95% on RT (22 reviews) and 7.5 from 62,011 IMDb votes.
7. **Ashes of Time** - His wuxia anti-epic, a swordsman's tale of memory and regret that reinvented the genre's look; evidence for the original is thin on RT, so the 2008 Redux figure (78% from 78 reviews) is shown separately.
8. **The Grandmaster** - A kung fu film about Ip Man and the end of an era, with fight choreography treated as portraiture; Oscar-nominated for cinematography and 78% from 139 reviews. The weakest IMDb score in the list (6.6), kept as his one large-scale later film.

### Credit, format and version flags

- **Ashes of Time (1994)**: Sole director per TMDB crew (Director, Screenplay). TMDB 40751 is the 1994 original. The 2008 re-edit 'Ashes of Time Redux' is a separate TMDB film (1610150) and a separate RT page (78% from 78 reviews).

**Near misses** (considered, not included):

- **As Tears Go By** (1988), TMDB 24163, IMDb tt0096461: His debut; IMDb 7.0 from 14,560 votes and RT 83% from 12 reviews; not stronger than Ashes of Time or The Grandmaster.
- **Ashes of Time Redux** (2008), TMDB 1610150: The 2008 re-edit; a different cut of Ashes of Time (#7), not ranked separately. The IMDb dataset has no separate title for the re-edit, so IMDb's single Ashes of Time entry (tt0109688) may blend votes for both cuts.
- **My Blueberry Nights** (2007), TMDB 1989, IMDb tt0765120: His English-language film; RT 47% from 122 reviews, IMDb 6.6 from 59,217 votes.
- **Eros (segment 'The Hand')** (2004), TMDB 39850, IMDb tt0343663: Anthology segment, co-credited with Steven Soderbergh and Michelangelo Antonioni; not a feature in its own right.
- **Other 2021-2025 TMDB entries (for example 'Hao Jiu Bu Jian' 1759201 and 'The Hand' 846716)**, TMDB 1759201: Not examined individually; they were not treated as eligible standard features.

## Ranking method

The method is the one set out in [`ESSENTIAL_10_EXAMPLE.md`](./ESSENTIAL_10_EXAMPLE.md#ranking-method) and was applied unchanged: eligibility from TMDB crew credits, an evidence panel of IMDb rating and votes, RT Tomatometer and review count and a note on directing, tiers by directing achievement, order within a tier by written editorial judgement, and the top three flagged as signature films. IMDb and RT are never averaged or combined and are used only as ordinal evidence within this director's own filmography. Suggested sample floors are 10,000 IMDb votes and 20 RT reviews; below those a score is shown but carries little weight. Two additions follow the owner's decisions: documentaries and co-directed films are allowed where essential and flagged, and the list is shorter than ten where ten would be padding.

### Where the evidence and the order disagree (all deliberate)

- **The list has eight films, not ten.** His feature filmography is 10 titles; As Tears Go By is a debut that does not clear the bar, and My Blueberry Nights (47% on RT) is below it. Eight is the honest number: adding either would be padding.
- **Chungking Express has IMDb 7.9 from 115,106 votes but RT 88% from 68 reviews, the lowest Tomatometer among his top four films.** It is #2 on influence and recognition; Days of Being Wild (91% from 32) and Fallen Angels (95% from 22) have better Tomatometers on smaller samples.
- **Ashes of Time and The Grandmaster (#7, #8) have the lowest IMDb ratings (7.0 and 6.6).** They are kept as the only remaining feature-length films with a clear directing case; they are the first to drop if the owner wants an even tighter list.

**Known uncertainties**

- RT pages are found under /m/in_the_mood_for_love_2001, /m/fallen_angels_hong_kong and /m/1083093-happy_together; the IMDb director name appears as 'Wong Kar-Wai' (nm0939182) and RT shows 'Kar-Wai Wong'.
- Ashes of Time: 1994 original (TMDB 40751) is ranked with IMDb 7.0; its RT page has only 3 reviews so RT is null for the original and the 2008 Redux page (78% from 78) is recorded separately. They are different cuts.
- IMDb has one Ashes of Time entry (tt0109688) with no separate Redux title, so its 7.0 / 18,532 votes may blend both cuts.
- TMDB credits for Wong include shorts, commercials, an anthology segment and several 2021-2025 entries (for example 'In the Mood for Love 2001', TMDB 1430370, which looks like a re-release listing) that were not examined individually; none was treated as a separate feature.
- Review counts and vote counts move daily; these are snapshots.
- TMDB film pages carry no IMDb link, so IMDb ids were matched by title, year and runtime; confirm via the TMDB API `external_ids` field when implementing.
- Alternate titles in the ledger come from IMDb primary and original titles and the TMDB display titles, not from TMDB's alternative-titles tab.
