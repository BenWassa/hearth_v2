## How identities were matched
Each film was searched on TMDB by title and release year. A match was accepted automatically only when the TMDB title and year both equalled the editorial title and year exactly. 49 of 50 matched that way. **Life (1999)** returned several same-year results and was chosen by hand (TMDB 6522, the Eddie Murphy / Martin Lawrence film). The six "Start here" films agree with the independently recorded IDs in `FEATURED_FILM_POSTER_SOURCES.md`. This is a lookup check, not a review: nobody has yet confirmed each entry against a second source.

## Inclusion status key
- **proposed**: taken from the BE source list; no one has reviewed its fit yet.
- **keep_for_now**: flagged as a borderline fit in the BE editorial audit (BE issue #2, section 3, P2): questions about genre (dramedy, romance/drama, musical/dance, stand-up concert film, action comedy). **Owner decision 2026-11-13: keep all 50 for now.** The page copy already says "comedies and comedy-adjacent stories". Revisit only if the collection is narrowed to strict comedy.

Chapter placement is a working taxonomy, not film history. Source positions from the BE list are deliberately not shown: they are not rankings.
