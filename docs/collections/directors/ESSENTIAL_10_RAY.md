### Rationale, one sentence each

1. **Pather Panchali** - His first film, made on a tiny budget with largely non-professional actors, that opened Indian cinema to the world; 98% from 82 reviews on RT and the highest-placed Ray in the 2022 Sight and Sound poll (35th).
2. **The World of Apu** - The close of the Apu trilogy and the highest IMDb rating in the ten (8.4); marriage, fatherhood and grief told as Apu's coming-of-age ends.
3. **Charulata** - A bored wife in 1879 Calcutta and her husband's cousin, filmed with a restraint and fluid camera work that make it his most polished chamber piece; Silver Bear for Best Director at Berlin 1965.
4. **Aparajito** - The middle of the trilogy, following Apu from Benares to Calcutta; Golden Lion at Venice 1957, which confirmed his international standing.
5. **The Music Room** - A decaying zamindar's last devotion to music, with the film's music and décor doing the dramatic work; 100% on RT from 23 reviews.
6. **Mahanagar** - A Calcutta housewife takes a job and changes her household; an early film about women at work, with the Silver Bear for Best Director at Berlin 1964.
7. **Devi** - A young wife is declared a goddess by her father-in-law, a quiet study of belief and its cost; 100% on RT from 15 reviews.
8. **Nayak** - A film star and a journalist on an overnight train; a near two-hander played largely in one confined setting, with Uttam Kumar in the lead; RT 100% but from only 10 reviews.
9. **Days and Nights in the Forest** - Four Calcutta friends on a forest holiday, an ensemble comedy of manners with 100% on RT from 23 reviews and a distinct late-period register.
10. **Distant Thunder** - The 1943 Bengal famine seen from one village, in colour; winner of the Golden Bear at Berlin 1973. The thinnest evidence in the ten (1,918 IMDb votes, 5 RT reviews), kept as the closest call (see below).

**Near misses** (considered, not included):

- **Teen Kanya (Three Daughters)** (1961), TMDB 43027, IMDb tt0055508: Three Tagore stories; IMDb 7.9 from 1,868 votes, not stronger than Distant Thunder on available evidence.
- **The Adventures of Goopy and Bagha** (1969), TMDB 135092, IMDb tt0063023: IMDb 8.6 from 6,134 votes but only 4 RT reviews and a children's fantasy register; first reserve for a tenth place.
- **The Home and the World** (1984), TMDB 42092, IMDb tt0087328: RT 100% from 12 reviews and a major literary adaptation; very close to Distant Thunder.
- **The Adversary (Pratidwandi)** (1970), TMDB 42498, IMDb tt0066237: IMDb 8.1 from 2,147 votes; the first of the Calcutta trilogy.
- **Jana Aranya (The Middleman)** (1975), TMDB 122445, IMDb tt0074710: IMDb 8.3 from 2,028 votes; RT 88% from 8 reviews (listed as 'The Masses' Music').
- **The Chess Players** (1977), TMDB 80556, IMDb tt0076696: His Hindi-language feature, 100% from 10 reviews; outside his core Bengali body of work.

## Ranking method

The method is the one set out in [`ESSENTIAL_10_EXAMPLE.md`](./ESSENTIAL_10_EXAMPLE.md#ranking-method) and was applied unchanged: eligibility from TMDB crew credits, an evidence panel of IMDb rating and votes, RT Tomatometer and review count and a note on directing, tiers by directing achievement, order within a tier by written editorial judgement, and the top three flagged as signature films. IMDb and RT are never averaged or combined and are used only as ordinal evidence within this director's own filmography. Suggested sample floors are 10,000 IMDb votes and 20 RT reviews; below those a score is shown but carries little weight. Two additions follow the owner's decisions: documentaries and co-directed films are allowed where essential and flagged, and the list is shorter than ten where ten would be padding.

### Where the evidence and the order disagree (all deliberate)

- **The three signature films are Pather Panchali, The World of Apu and Charulata, not the three Apu films.** Aparajito (IMDb 8.2, RT 96% from 23 reviews, Golden Lion) is #4: the trilogy is best taken together, but Charulata has the stronger standing as a standalone masterwork (Silver Bear for Best Director, 2022 Sight and Sound poll at =169). If the owner prefers 'the Apu trilogy' as 1 to 3, move Charulata to #4.
- **Evidence is thin across most of the list.** Only Pather Panchali (42,593), The World of Apu (20,935) and Aparajito (20,539) clear the 10,000-vote floor; Charulata has 8,180 and Nayak 6,382. This is a reflection of how little audience data exists for Indian-language classics, not of their standing. Ranks rest more heavily on critical history and awards than on the scores.
- **Mahanagar (#6) and Nayak (#8) have IMDb 8.3, higher than The Music Room (#5) and Devi (#7), both 7.8.** Reason: all four have small samples (Mahanagar 5,797 votes, Nayak 6,382, The Music Room 7,515, Devi 3,606), so the order follows the directing case and awards rather than the decimals. The Adventures of Goopy and Bagha (8.6) is a near miss because it has only 4 RT reviews and is a children's fantasy.

**Known uncertainties**

- Evidence is thin for most of the ten (see above). Distant Thunder's 100% on RT is from 5 reviews and should not be treated as a Tomatometer in any display.
- IMDb ids were matched by title, year and runtime; The Music Room is 100 minutes on TMDB and 95 on IMDb, Devi 99 and 99, all in a plausible range.
- RT pages for Devi and Distant Thunder have US re-release years; the ledger uses the original release year.
- The Berlin awards were verified in a web search (Wikipedia lists and satyajitray.org); a second search found one source dating Charulata's Silver Bear to 1964, the others to 1965. The ledger says 1965.
- Ray's Golden Lion for Aparajito (Venice 1957) and the Golden Bear for Distant Thunder (Berlin 1973) were confirmed in the same search.
- Review counts and vote counts move daily; these are snapshots.
- TMDB film pages carry no IMDb link, so IMDb ids were matched by title, year and runtime; confirm via the TMDB API `external_ids` field when implementing.
- Alternate titles in the ledger come from IMDb primary and original titles and the TMDB display titles, not from TMDB's alternative-titles tab.
