## How to review

Review each fixture against the title as a whole and its supplied public metadata. Assess each facet independently; do not infer one facet from another. For every facet, either approve the listed label or write a revised label in the owner decision column. The issue’s gate requires explicit human approval or revision of all 50 real fixture labels, including resolution of hard-rule conflicts. Rollout remains blocked until that gate is met.

- **Comfort** — Overall emotional ease: low means predominantly tense, harsh or distressing; medium means mixed but broadly warm/easy; high means predominantly gentle, reassuring or feel-good.
- **Comedy first** — How joke-forward the experience is: low means comedy is incidental; medium means substantial comedy mixed with another primary mode; high means comedy is a principal reason to watch.
- **Plot momentum** — Narrative propulsion: low means loose, episodic or contemplative; medium means clear forward progression without constant urgency; high means sustained goal, chase, investigation, survival or similarly propulsive structure.
- **Narrative complexity** — Structure and tracking, not subject matter: low means straightforward and mostly linear; medium means some parallel threads, structural or technical tracking; high means intricate, nonlinear, densely interwoven or deliberately difficult to track.
- **Visual distinctiveness** — Visual craft: low means broadly conventional; medium means noticeably stylized, polished or visually authored; high means exceptional signature cinematography, production design or animation. Budget and genre alone are not evidence.
- **Attention demand** — Concentration needed to follow the title: low means easy to follow with low cognitive load; medium means intermittent concentration or moderate detail/intensity; high means sustained dense tracking, concentration or intensity. Runtime alone is not evidence.

**Unknown** means the available evidence cannot distinguish a level; preserve it when evidence is insufficient rather than guessing. The evaluator’s deterministic `unknown` is an abstention, not evidence that the fixture label is wrong.
